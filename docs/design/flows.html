IYT Test · Telegram mini app + website · hi-fi user flows · 02.11.2026

Practice questions for IYT certification levels

A student picks a level (Bareboat Skipper first), practises by topic with an explanation after every answer, then sits a timed mock exam. Questions are situations — what you see, what the instruments say, where the other boat is — drawn as figures. Mistakes are collected for review; mastery per topic drives what to practise next. The website shows the same bank with a landing page per level and short theory notes for every topic.

LookWebsite: Merriweather Light display, Carlito caps kickers, one blue accent, 28px bands. Mini app: the same tokens in Telegram mini-app chrome (app.css), day and night.
Bankdata/questions/bareboat-skipper.json: 100 scenario questions, 10 topics × 10, 62 with a drawn figure, four options, a one-line note and reference. All of them: questions.html. Levels next: International Crew, Yachtmaster Coastal, Offshore.
ModesPractice (feedback after each question, any topics, 10/20/all) · Mock exam (40 questions, 60 min, pass 70%, feedback at the end) · Review mistakes · Daily question in the bot.
Colour codeCorrect — accent blue; wrong — red; flagged — yellow dot; mastered — black. No green anywhere (the palette has none), so a correct answer reads by the check mark as well as the colour.

Buoyage & lights

A south cardinal mark shows which light rhythm?

Nine quick flashes
Six quick flashes followed by one long flash

Think of a clock face: north — continuous; east — 3; south — 6 + long flash; west — 9.

Buoyage & lights

A buoy with red and white vertical stripes and a single red sphere topmark is a…

isolated danger mark
safe water mark, e.g. a landfall or mid-channel buoy

Safe water: navigable water all round. Its light is white — isophase, occulting, one long flash every 10 s or Morse "A".

How it works

1

Practise by topic

Ten topics per level. Situations, not definitions: you see the figure and decide. A one-line note names the rule.

2

Sit a mock exam

Forty random questions, sixty minutes, no feedback until you submit — like the real thing.

3

Clear your mistakes

Wrong answers come back until you get them right twice. Mastery per topic shows what to do next.

Theory notes

The rules behind every question

Each topic has a short note — COLREGs rules, the IALA system, compass conversion, the Beaufort scale — written to be read in ten minutes before practising. Every explanation links back to its note.
