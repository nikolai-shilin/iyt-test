Helm and Drone

Every picture is shot from one of two cameras. Helm: what the skipper sees from the wheel, with your own bow in the foreground. Drone: a tilted view from above, for situations that need the whole picture.

  • Built to match photographsEvery colour, the haze and the black and white points come from measured photographs of the same kind of view; nothing photographed is in the picture, so nothing can end up in front of a rigging line or behind a ship.
  • Exact where it countsBuoy shapes, colours and topmarks, light positions and arcs come from the rules, so a picture cannot show an impossible combination.
  • Few wordsLabels are small pills, used only in drone view. In helm view the picture stays clean, as you'd see it.
  • Its own skyEach picture brings its own sky and sea, like a photo, so it reads the same in the day and night themes.
