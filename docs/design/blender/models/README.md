## Import notes (checked in Blender 5.2, 2026-11-05)

Ready to use as they are: `buoy-lateral-red` and `life-jacket` (Poly Haven, photo-scanned), `lifebuoy`, `liferaft-canister`,
`tender-rib`, `vessel-motor-yacht`, `buoy-lighted-uscg`, `cleat-deck`, `winch-harken-32`, `anchor-cqr`, `anchor-delta`, `bollard-quay`.

Need fixing before use:
- Wrong units: `jeanneau-45` and `sailboat-24ft` scale x2.54; `winch-lewmar-st` scale /25.4 (two winches in the file).
- Extra scenery to delete: the four `buoy-cardinal-*` and `vessel-ferry` sit on a large sea disc; `vessel-trawler` on a slab;
  `vessel-tug` has wake and sea objects; `fenders` is a spread-out scene (keep one fender); `anchor-delta-flipper` has two anchors.
- `sun-odyssey-49ds` flies a spinnaker; hide it for most figures. `catamaran-42` likewise has a striped gennaker.
- Most SketchUp models lose their colours in the GLB export and come in white or grey: swap materials (common.scene_materials()).
- `vessel-cargo` came out as a partial, odd-looking ship; treat it as a placeholder.
